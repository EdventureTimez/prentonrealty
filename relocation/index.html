How It Works

A Soft Landing, Managed End to End

Relocating is a dozen decisions stacked on a deadline, usually a start date. Our job is to compress months of local learning into days: which neighborhoods fit your commute and your family, how District 203 and 204 schools actually compare, what your money buys here versus where you are coming from, and what has to happen, in what order, before the movers arrive.

Most of our relocation clients buy or lease before they ever set foot in Illinois. We run the search remotely with live video tours, give you the honest read a listing photo will not, coordinate inspections and closing while you are still abroad or out of state, and have the utilities and keys sorted for the day you land.

Questions

Relocation Questions

Can you help me buy a home in Naperville before I arrive?

Yes. We run remote searches with video tours, handle inspections and closing logistics, and have helped buyers purchase from other states and other countries before ever landing in Chicago.

Do you work with corporate relocation and HR teams?

Yes. We build referral relationships with employers moving staff into the area, with a single point of contact, consistent reporting, and the same principal-level service for a vice president or a new graduate.

I am moving internationally. Can you still help with financing?

We connect relocating clients with lenders experienced in visa-holder and newly-arrived-buyer financing, and we coordinate the paperwork across time zones so the move date never slips because of the mortgage.

What if I want to rent first?

Smart move in many cases. We help transferees lease first and buy later, and because we already know your situation, the eventual purchase is faster and better informed.
