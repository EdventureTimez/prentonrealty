How It Works

From Search to Signed Lease

Most business owners negotiate a lease a handful of times in their lives. Landlords negotiate them every month. Tenant representation exists to level that table, and because the commission is typically built into the deal and paid by the landlord, you get senior-level representation without writing a check.

We recently negotiated a five-year retail lease for a first-time operator that included five months of total rent abatement and a landlord-funded refresh of the unit before move-in. Terms like those do not appear in the listing. They get negotiated, in writing, by someone who knows what the market is conceding.

Questions

Tenant Representation Questions

Is tenant representation really free for the tenant?

In most Chicagoland lease deals the landlord pays the brokerage commission, which is shared with the tenant’s broker. You get representation, and the cost structure of the deal does not change.

I already found a space I like. Is it too late?

No, as long as you have not signed. Bring us in before you submit a letter of intent and we negotiate the economics and the lease language on your behalf.

How much rent abatement can a tenant get?

It depends on the market, the condition of the space, and the length of the term, but concessions are very real in the current market. Several months of free rent on a multi-year lease is a common outcome of a well-negotiated deal.

Do you help with renewals?

Yes. Renewals are where landlords quietly make their margin. We benchmark your renewal against the open market, often twelve months before expiration, so you renew from strength or move with time to spare.
