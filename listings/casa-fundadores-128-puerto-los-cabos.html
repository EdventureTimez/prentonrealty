Overview

Casa Fundadores 128

A new six-bedroom estate on a fairway lot with Sea of Cortez views, designed by Yturbe Taller de Arquitectura. It is under construction, and the buyer selects the finishes.

A cactus garden leads to a courtyard with an outdoor fireplace. Past it, a beamed great room opens to the terrace, where the infinity pool faces the sea. The materials are stucco, local stone, travertine, and fine woods.

The plan has six bedrooms including two primary suites, and one bedroom can be converted to a flex room. There is a Wolf and Sub-Zero kitchen plus a service kitchen, a sauna, a jacuzzi, a firepit, a grill kitchen, and a garage for two cars and a golf cart.

Because the home is still under construction, its buyer chooses the finishes. Showings are private and by appointment through the listing brokers.

Images are renderings. The home is under construction, and finishes and details may change.
