Questions

Naperville Commercial Real Estate Questions

Does tenant representation cost the tenant anything?

In most Naperville lease transactions the landlord pays the brokerage commission, so tenant representation typically costs the tenant nothing. You get a broker negotiating rent, abatement, build-out, and terms on your side of the table.

What areas does Prenton Realty cover?

We are based in Naperville and work across the western suburbs and Chicagoland, including Aurora, Lisle, Downers Grove, Wheaton, Warrenville, Bolingbrook, and the I-88 and I-55 corridors, as well as the city of Chicago.

What property types do you handle?

Restaurants and hospitality, retail, office and medical office, industrial and warehouse, multifamily, and special-purpose properties such as gyms, schools, and religious centers.

Can you help me understand what my commercial property is worth?

Yes. We prepare broker opinions of value with real comparable data and honest assumptions, whether you are considering a sale, a refinance, or simply planning ahead.
