Common Questions

Investment groups ask us

We’re a group of partners pooling money. Can you work with us?

Yes — that is exactly who this practice serves. We plug in as your real estate execution arm: sourcing, underwriting, negotiation, and closing coordination, while your attorney and CPA structure the entity. One point of contact for the whole group.

What asset types do you cover?

Multifamily, industrial and flex, NNN retail, mixed-use, hospitality, and land across Chicagoland — plus international opportunities through our Los Cabos platform and referral coverage in South Florida and Texas.

How are you paid?

Success-based brokerage compensation at closing in most mandates — aligned with your outcome. Commissions are set by agreement and fully negotiable. Underwriting-only engagements are scoped case by case.

What does the first conversation look like?

Thirty confidential minutes: your capital base, target returns, hold horizon, and risk appetite. You leave with a candid read on what the market can actually deliver — and whether we’re the right fit.
