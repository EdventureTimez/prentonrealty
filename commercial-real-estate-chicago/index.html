Questions

Chicago Commercial Questions

Do you work with first-time commercial investors?

Yes. Many of our clients are buying their first apartment building or leasing their first commercial space. We walk you through underwriting, financing options, due diligence, and closing, in plain language.

Can you represent my business in a lease negotiation if I have already found the space?

Yes. Even when a tenant has identified a space, a broker adds leverage on rent, abatement, tenant improvement allowances, and lease terms, and the landlord typically pays the commission.

Do you handle 1031 exchanges?

Yes. Our Capital Markets practice builds 1031 timelines and sources replacement properties, coordinating with your attorney and qualified intermediary.
